On two wheels

First question: where does it sleep tonight?

Anyone travelling on two wheels knows it: you recognise the right place by whether it has proper storage. Here it does — covered, on request, motorbikes included — and around it there is a whole apartment on the Sacro Cuore hill, with the historic centre of Ascoli ten to fifteen minutes away on foot and the Sibillini Mountains half an hour by road.

Frequently asked questions

What riders ask us most often.

Storage

Is the bike storage indoors?

Yes: covered storage available on request. Bikes, e-bikes and motorbikes spend the night under cover, not on the street.

Rental

Do you rent e-bikes directly?

We arrange it with local partners: send us dates and sizes on WhatsApp and everything is ready when you arrive.

Laundry

Can I wash my cycling kit?

Yes: washing machine included in the apartment, with an iron.

On a motorbike

Does it work for motorbike travellers?

Yes: the covered storage on request takes motorbikes too, and the area sits outside the restricted traffic zone, so you come and go freely.

Early starts

Can I have breakfast before sunrise?

Yes: the kitchen is fully equipped and yours alone, and check-in and check-out are self-service.

Languages

Do you speak English?

Honestly, Giovanni and Daniela's English is a work in progress and their German is mostly smiles and good intentions — but between a translation app, a few words and a lot of warmth we always find a way to understand each other. Write to us, you'll see.
